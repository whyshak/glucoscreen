Why early detection matters most

In health screening, the cost of a missed risk is much higher than a false alarm. A false alarm simply prompts a routine checkup, whereas a missed case could delay life-changing lifestyle interventions.

Clinical Design Goal: Our model is deliberately calibrated to prioritize a high 83.0% sensitivity rate, keeping the miss rate down to only 17.0%.
High-Risk Sensitivity (Recall) 82.95%
Catches ~83 out of 100 potential diabetes cases early.
Miss Rate (False Negative Rate) 17.05%
Intentionally minimized so very few elevated risks are overlooked.

Machine learning architecture

GlucoScreen runs on a non-linear Radial Basis Function (RBF) Support Vector Machine (SVM) combined with standardized scaling. It was trained and evaluated on balanced CDC health surveillance survey records.

Generalization Power: An ROC-AUC of 82.9% and an F1 Score of 77.0% confirm the model performs reliably across diverse ages, BMIs, and backgrounds without overfitting.
ROC Area Under Curve (ROC-AUC) 82.90%
Measures the model's ability to separate distinct risk classes.
Harmonic Balanced Score (F1 Score) 76.97%
Harmonious balance between detection sensitivity and alert precision.
Overall Test Accuracy (Accuracy) 75.18%
Overall proportion of correct predictions across all test cases.
Prescreening vs. Clinical Diagnosis: GlucoScreen is an informational risk prescreening questionnaire that calculates statistical likelihood from self-reported CDC health indicators. It does not replace diagnostic blood tests (like HbA1c or Fasting Blood Glucose). We encourage sharing your results with a licensed healthcare professional.
